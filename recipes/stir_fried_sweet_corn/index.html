    Stir-Fried Sweet Corn | 清炒甜玉米¶

    Quick Info | 快速信息 - ⏱️ Prep Time | 准备时间: 5 minutes | 5分钟 - 🔥 Cook Time | 烹饪时间: 5 minutes | 5分钟 - 🍽️ Servings | 份量: 1 serving | 1人份 - 📊 Difficulty | 难度: Easy | 简单

    📝 Description | 菜品介绍¶

    English:
    This classic Chinese-style stir-fried sweet corn highlights the natural sweetness of fresh corn kernels. With only salt as seasoning, it's a quick, healthy, and delicious side dish that takes just 10 minutes to prepare. The corn stays tender-crisp and retains its juicy flavor.

    中文：
    这道经典中式清炒甜玉米突出了新鲜玉米粒的天然甜味。仅用盐调味，是一道快速、健康、美味的配菜，仅需10分钟即可完成。玉米保持嫩脆口感，保留多汁风味。

    🥘 Ingredients | 食材清单¶

    Ingredient 食材 Quantity 分量 Notes 备注
    Fresh Sweet Corn Kernels 新鲜甜玉米粒 200g 200克 From 1-2 ears of corn 来自1-2根玉米棒
    Salt 盐 2g 2克 To taste 适量
    Cooking Oil 食用油 10ml 10毫升 Vegetable oil 植物油
    Water 水 20ml 20毫升 For steaming 用于蒸煮

    Notes on Measurements | 分量说明: - All measurements are precise in grams (g) or milliliters (ml) for consistency
    所有分量均以克(g)或毫升(ml)为单位，保持一致性 - One ear of corn yields approximately 100-120g of kernels
    一根玉米棒约产出100-120克玉米粒 - Use fresh corn for best sweetness and texture
    使用新鲜玉米以获得最佳甜度和口感

    👨‍🍳 Instructions | 制作步骤¶

    Step 1 | 第一步: Prepare the Corn | 准备玉米¶

    English:
    If using whole corn cobs, remove the kernels by cutting downward with a sharp knife. Rinse the kernels under cold water and drain well. If using frozen corn, thaw completely and pat dry.

    中文：
    如果使用整根玉米棒，用刀向下切去除玉米粒。在冷水下冲洗玉米粒并充分沥干。如果使用冷冻玉米，完全解冻并拍干。

    💡 Tip | 小贴士:
    To easily remove kernels, stand the cob upright on a cutting board and slice downward.
    将玉米棒直立在砧板上，向下切片可轻松去除玉米粒。

    Step 2 | 第二步: Heat the Pan | 热锅¶

    English:
    Place a wok or frying pan over medium-high heat. Add 10ml of cooking oil and swirl to coat the bottom evenly. Heat for about 20 seconds until the oil is warm.

    中文：
    将炒锅或煎锅置于中大火上。加入10毫升食用油，晃动使油均匀覆盖锅底。加热约20秒至油温热。

    ⚠️ Important | 注意事项:
    Don't let the oil smoke, as high heat can burn the delicate corn kernels.
    不要让油冒烟，因为高温会烧焦娇嫩的玉米粒。

    Step 3 | 第三步: Stir-Fry the Corn | 翻炒玉米¶

    English:
    Add the corn kernels to the hot oil and stir-fry continuously for 2-3 minutes until they start to turn slightly golden and fragrant. Keep stirring to prevent sticking.

    中文：
    将玉米粒加入热油中，持续翻炒2-3分钟至开始略微金黄并散发香味。不断搅拌防止粘锅。

    🌟 Pro Tip | 专业技巧:
    Continuous stirring ensures even cooking and prevents the corn from burning.
    持续搅拌确保均匀受热并防止玉米烧焦。

    Step 4 | 第四步: Steam and Season | 蒸煮与调味¶

    English:
    Add 20ml of water to the pan and immediately cover with a lid. Let it steam for 1 minute to ensure the corn is cooked through. Remove the lid, add 2g of salt, and stir-fry for another 30 seconds until the water evaporates.

    中文：
    向锅中加入20毫升水，立即盖上锅盖。蒸煮1分钟以确保玉米熟透。打开锅盖，加入2克盐，再翻炒30秒至水分蒸发。

    💡 Tip | 小贴士:
    The brief steaming keeps the corn tender while the quick stir-frying maintains its crispness.
    短暂蒸煮保持玉米嫩滑，快速翻炒保持其脆爽。

    Step 5 | 第五步: Plate and Serve | 装盘上桌¶

    English:
    Transfer the stir-fried corn to a serving plate. Serve immediately while hot to enjoy its natural sweetness and tender-crisp texture.

    中文：
    将清炒玉米装入盘中。趁热立即食用以享受其天然甜味和嫩脆口感。

    🔄 Variations | 变化做法¶

    Option 1 | 做法一: Butter Version | 黄油版
    Replace oil with 10g of butter for a richer flavor.
    用10克黄油替换食用油，获得更浓郁的风味。

    Option 2 | 做法二: Garlic Version | 蒜香版
    Add 5g of minced garlic when heating the oil.
    热油时加入5克蒜末。

    Option 3 | 做法三: Herb Version | 香草版
    Sprinkle 1g of dried parsley before serving.
    装盘前撒1克干欧芹。

    📌 Storage Tips | 储存建议¶

    • Refrigerator | 冷藏: Store in an airtight container for up to 2 days
      放入密封容器中冷藏，最多保存2天

    • Reheating | 重新加热: Reheat in a pan over medium heat for 1-2 minutes
      用中火在锅中重新加热1-2分钟

    • Freezing | 冷冻: Can be frozen for up to 1 month
      可冷冻保存最多1个月

    • Best enjoyed fresh | 最好现做现吃: For optimal sweetness, consume within 24 hours
      为获得最佳甜度，建议在24小时内食用
